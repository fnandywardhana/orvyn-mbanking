ORVYN 0.4.0

What's new
- Open an account from the app: confirm your phone with a code, tell us about yourself, take a KTP photo and a selfie (they never leave your phone), choose a password and PIN, then wait for the ORVYN team to approve.
- Inbox: the bell on the home screen shows money in and out, card payments (and why one was declined), and messages from the ORVYN team.
- Banners on the home screen, and announcements when the team has news.
- Profile: change your PIN or password, pick light, dark or match-your-phone, read questions and answers, and see exactly what ORVYN can and can't access in Privacy.
- Share a receipt as a picture from any transaction.
- Rename or remove people in your saved recipients.

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
