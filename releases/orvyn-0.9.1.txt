ORVYN 0.9.1

What's new
- Requests and split bills use the same minimum as transfers, Rp10,000. Before, you could ask for less, and your friend then couldn't pay it. A request that's already open can still be paid.
- Splitting a bill shows which shares are below the minimum, and a friend you add by account number stays on the list when you go back.
- Insights: a category's detail now lists your credit card spending too, so the list adds up to the total.
- Fixed: paying out a time deposit that earned no interest no longer fails.
- Done on the keyboard runs the screen's button on every form.
- With large text, buttons that sit side by side stack instead of breaking words, and request rows stay readable.
- The offline message no longer covers the QRIS button, and TalkBack reads card numbers as "card ending 1234".

Permissions this app asks for
No change from 0.9.0.
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code and account activity (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- RECEIVE (Firebase), WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Firebase so push notifications arrive. None of them reads anything about you or your phone.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
- RECORD_AUDIO: only while you hold Vyna's microphone open, on phones that can recognise speech by themselves. Your voice never leaves the phone. Android asks the first time.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
