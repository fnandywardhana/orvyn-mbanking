ORVYN 0.9.0

What's new
- Large text works everywhere. At the biggest font size on a small phone, the PIN pad, the amount keypad and the Done button always fit on screen, and balances never break in the middle of a number.
- Nothing fails quietly. If something doesn't go through, the app says so and offers Try again. While your PIN is being checked, the pad shows it's working. Withdrawing a deposit early now has its own review and success screens.
- Share receipt right after you send money, pay by QRIS, pay a bill or pay a request. Every success screen shows the date and time.
- Tap your account on the home screen to see its details, then share your account number in one tap.
- History has Date and Type filters, and you can pull it down to refresh.
- Forgot PIN? Sign in with your password and choose a new one.
- Wrong PINs in Change PIN and Change password count toward the same 5 tries as everywhere else.
- Sign-up tells you about a NIK that's already registered, or a PIN that's too easy to guess, on the step where you type it, not at the end.
- My QR can ask for an amount. Mobile top-up fills in your own number. You can buy gold in grams.
- A flashlight in the QRIS scanner.
- Promos now sit below your recent transactions.
- Clearer chips in dark mode, one typeface throughout, better contrast, and TalkBack reads every switch as on or off.

Permissions this app asks for
No change from 0.8.1. The flashlight uses the camera permission you already give for scanning.
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code and account activity (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- RECEIVE (Firebase), WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Firebase so push notifications arrive. None of them reads anything about you or your phone.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
- RECORD_AUDIO: only while you hold Vyna's microphone open, on phones that can recognise speech by themselves. Your voice never leaves the phone. Android asks the first time.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
