ORVYN 0.3.0

What's new
- Transfer: send to other ORVYN customers instantly and free, or to other banks by BI-FAST (Rp2,500) or RTGS for amounts above Rp100,000,000 (Rp25,000). You see the account holder's name before sending. Save people you pay often.
- QRIS: scan a merchant's QRIS with your camera or pick a QR picture from your gallery, then pay with your PIN. "My QR" lets other ORVYN customers scan you to send money.
- Pay and buy: PLN tokens and bills, BPJS Kesehatan, mobile credit and data, and GoPay, OVO, DANA and ShopeePay top-ups. Numbers you paid before are one tap away.
- Cards: your debit card and, for some testers, an ORVYN credit card. Freeze a card, turn online, overseas and contactless payments on or off, set a daily limit, see the full card number after your PIN, and pay your credit card bill.

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while the QRIS scanner is open. The QR code is read on your phone; no pictures are taken or sent.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads. Pictures you pick from the gallery stay on your phone.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
