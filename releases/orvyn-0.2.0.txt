ORVYN 0.2.0

What's new
- Sign in with the phone number and password from the ORVYN team.
- Create your own 6-digit PIN. Next time, open ORVYN with your PIN or fingerprint.
- Home: total balance (tap the eye to hide it), your account, and recent transactions.
- History: six months of transactions, grouped by day. Search by name, note or amount, and filter money in or out.
- Tap any transaction for its details and reference number.
- ORVYN signs you out after 5 minutes without use.

Coming next: transfers, QRIS, bill payments and cards.

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in. Android checks your fingerprint; ORVYN only learns whether it matched.
No contacts, location, SMS, files, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to install
1. Open the download link on your Android phone and download the APK.
2. Tap the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
3. Tap Install (or Update), then Open.
