ORVYN 0.7.0

What's new
- Vyna, your assistant: tap the sparkle at the top of the home screen. Ask about your balance, spending or anything in ORVYN, in English or Bahasa Indonesia. She can get a transfer or a pocket move ready, and you always check it and enter your PIN yourself. She can read her replies aloud.
- Insights: where your money went this month by category, six months at a glance, and how this month compares with the same days last month. Open it from the card on the home screen or from History.
- Budgets: set a monthly limit per category and get a reminder at 80% and 100%. Nothing is ever blocked.
- Change the category of any payment from its details.
- E-statements: a PDF of any of the last 12 months, under Profile.
- Home screen widget: Scan QRIS in one tap, and peek at your balance for 10 seconds.

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code and account activity (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- RECEIVE (Firebase), WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Firebase so push notifications arrive. None of them reads anything about you or your phone.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
- RECORD_AUDIO: only while you hold Vyna's microphone open, on phones that can recognise speech by themselves. Your voice never leaves the phone. Android asks the first time.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
