ORVYN 0.8.0

What's new
- Wealth: everything you have with ORVYN in one number, with a bar showing how it's split. Open it from the Wealth card on the home screen.
- Foreign currency: hold USD, SGD, EUR, JPY or AUD and exchange at the rates shown. Prestige customers get better rates.
- Time deposits: lock money away for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a fixed rate, see the interest after tax before you confirm, and choose to renew or pay out at maturity.
- Investments: take a five-question risk quiz, then buy made-up mutual funds whose prices move every day, retail government bonds that pay a coupon every month, or gold by the gram.
- Prestige: gold touches on the home screen, and your Relationship Manager's card with one-tap Call and Message.
- Passkeys: add one in Profile, then sign in on a new phone with your fingerprint or screen lock instead of your password.
- Fixed: the balance on the home screen could be a few rupiah off for very large balances while it counted up.
- Fixed: signing in for the first time asks you to create a PIN (also in 0.7.1).

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code and account activity (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- RECEIVE (Firebase), WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Firebase so push notifications arrive. None of them reads anything about you or your phone.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
- RECORD_AUDIO: only while you hold Vyna's microphone open, on phones that can recognise speech by themselves. Your voice never leaves the phone. Android asks the first time.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
