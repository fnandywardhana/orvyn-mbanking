ORVYN 0.6.0

What's new
- Pockets: put money aside for a trip, a laptop or a rainy day. Set a target and a date, move money in and out for free, and see your progress on the home screen.
- Auto-save: a pocket can fill itself with a share of every salary, a set amount every week, or the change from each payment rounded up to Rp10,000.
- Scheduled transfers: on the review screen, pick Schedule to send later, every week or every month. Your PIN covers every send. See, pause or delete them under Transfer, Scheduled.
- Request money: ask anyone on ORVYN to pay you back. They get a notification and pay with one tap and their PIN.
- Split a bill: share a bill with up to 9 friends, evenly or your way, and see who has paid. Open any QRIS, bill or card payment and tap Split this bill.
- Fixed: paying someone from your recent list again now shows their real name.

Permissions this app asks for
- INTERNET: to talk to the ORVYN server.
- CAMERA: only while scanning a QR code or taking the sign-up photos. Nothing is uploaded.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show your sign-up code and account activity (Android 13 and newer ask first).
- RECEIVE (Firebase), WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Firebase so push notifications arrive. None of them reads anything about you or your phone.
- USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: only if you turn on fingerprint sign-in.
No contacts, location, SMS, storage, device IDs, analytics or ads.

How to update
Open the download link on your phone, download, tap the file, then Update.
